The [V3 dataset release](research/data-release/README.md) publishes canonical
claims, source sentences, selected embedding matrices and entity-resolution
tables under its [Research Data Evaluation License](research/data-release/LICENSE.txt).
That grant applies only to the original compilation and materials in which
Conrad Compagna holds rights. Underlying source texts and other third-party
materials retain their own status and applicable terms; no exclusive rights
are claimed over public-domain material or historical facts.

The dataset license does not relicense the application, upstream software or
external resources. Dependency manifests and existing component license
declarations continue to apply.
